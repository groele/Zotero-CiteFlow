# Zotero CiteFlow v13.0.10 Release Notes

**Release Date**: 2026-11-03  
**Version**: `13.0.10`  
**Compatibility**: Zotero `10.0` – `12.*`

## Highlights

1. **Modular Preferences Architecture & 40px Golden Alignment (`chrome/content/preferences.xhtml`, `chrome/content/preferences.css`)**:
   - Refactored settings panel into 5 distinct business workflow modules: Core Tracking Service, Writing Interaction & Quick Cite, Collections & Tag Governance, Scheduling & Performance, and Diagnostics & Maintenance, plus an authoritative Version Card.
   - Strictly conforms to `AGENTS.md` 4.1 & 4.2 typography and alignment standards: standard 14px control font size, 16px horizontal card padding, and exact 40px vertical golden alignment line for secondary controls and tertiary subordinate descriptions/inputs.
2. **Dedicated Temporary Collections Toggle & Dependency Interlocking (`prefs.js`, `src/modules/preferences.mjs`, `src/modules/citation/collections.mjs`, `src/modules/citation/preferences-sync.mjs`)**:
   - Added `extensions.zotero.zoterocitation.collections.enable` preference and setting checkbox, granting users full control over whether Word document temporary collection trees are generated.
   - Implemented dynamic dependency protection (`updateDependencies`): disabling "Maintain citation tags" automatically disables and visually dims the subordinate tag text input field, preventing invalid or ambiguous configurations.
   - Robust live preference updates: toggling collection creation dynamically cleans up or re-initializes temporary collections for active editing sessions.
3. **Comprehensive Localization (`locale/zh-CN`, `locale/en-US`)**:
   - Full bilingual coverage across all 5 setting modules, section summaries, and help tooltips with complete consistency.
4. **Source Code Cleanliness**:
   - Strictly maintained all 27 source modules under `<= 360` lines of code.
